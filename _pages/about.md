I'm an applied mathematician. I work on measurement: Bayesian inference, calibration,
uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis and hypothesis testing — first on
physical models (EDF R&D, IFP Energies nouvelles / ONERA), now on AI models and
public-health data.

What interests me: getting inside models, and producing numbers about them that hold up
under outside scrutiny. Every post here asks one question: how would I know whether to
trust this?

Recent work: [a calibration bench for secret-loyalty detectors](https://github.com/Edimah/secret-loyalties)
(Apart Research, July 2026) · [detecting an LLM-agent swarm in public edit logs](https://github.com/Edimah/agentic-collusion-detector-in-public-logs)
(Apart Research × CeSIA, September 2026).
