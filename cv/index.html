Detecting agentic collusion in public logs with statistical methods

Apart Research × CeSIA sprint, Sept 2026

Track 2. 14,591 reconstructed agent revisions and 32,656 live edits over four wikis, 2003–2026. A ladder of four null models, up to a difference-in-differences against an untouched sibling wiki with a placebo-in-time test: the incident weeks exceed all 4,872 reference weeks, the effect is 3.2 log points, the change point falls on 16 June. A weekly check built from the same features raises no false alarm over the 2,236 wiki-weeks of the preceding decade.

github.com/Edimah/agentic-collusion-detector-in-public-logs

A Calibrated Two-Level Detector for Secret Loyalties

Apart Research × Formation Research sprint, July 2026

With A. Randrianandrasana. My contributions: estimator, permutation test, calibration bench, difference-in-differences, control selection, activation probe. Calibration-matched controls cut false positives from 14/16 to 2/16 on a clean model, where every positive is false by construction. The instrument specification was written before any result existed and kept as a pre-registration, its result fields never back-filled. Reviewer: “This is rigorous work, and the calibration is a key contribution here.”

Modelling MNAR data with population data #1 : Why is this cell blank?

Blog post, July 2026

Suppression in published cancer-registry tables reads the count it hides, which makes the missingness not-at-random. Across tenths of expected case count the share of withheld cells runs from 93% to 0%; across tenths of population, from 13% to 1%.
