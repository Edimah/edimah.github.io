False collusion alerts under incomplete information

AI Collusion Research Sprint, Apart Research, solo, 23–25 October 2026

  • In a simulated market, a standard screen’s false-accusation rate rises 5.8% → 100% as auditor information degrades.
  • A simple analytical model predicts these rates closely, without being fitted to them.
  • Then proposed a “re-asking” test that bypasses the agents’ secret channels.
  • Exploratory pilot: 0/9 false flags, 3/3 planted pairs detected, with a statistic chosen after inspection.

Hypothesis Testing, Sensitivity Analysis, Mathematical Modelling, Simulation, Python

Detecting agentic collusion in public logs with statistical methods

AI Incident Response Sprint, Apart Research × CeSIA, Track 2, solo, September 2026

  • Used statistical methods to detect an LLM-agent swarm from public edit logs alone.
  • Reconstructed the logs from two public sources over 2003–2026.
  • Designed a ladder of four null models to characterise the incident and its statistical significance.
  • Built a weekly check as the incident-response deliverable.
  • It flags the three largest incident weeks and none of the 2,236 earlier wiki-weeks (2016–2025).

A Calibrated Two-Level Detector for Secret Loyalties – Application to an LLM

“Secret Loyalties” hackathon, Apart Research × Formation Research, team of 2, July 2026

  • Designed an evaluation framework, then measured its false-positive rate on a clean model.
  • Using matched-control calibration, cut false positives from 12/16 to 2/16.
  • Result: top 25% of 179 submissions; one reviewer cited the calibration as “a key contribution”.
